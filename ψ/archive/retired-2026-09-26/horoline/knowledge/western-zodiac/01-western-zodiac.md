## Elements & Modalities

**Elements**: Fire (passion/creativity), Earth (stability/accumulation), Air (communication/change), Water (emotion/intuition)

**Modalities**: Cardinal (initiate), Fixed (stabilize), Mutable (adapt)

## Compatibility (Key Patterns)

- **Trines** (same element, 120°): Natural harmony — Aries/Leo/Sagittarius, Taurus/Virgo/Capricorn, Gemini/Libra/Aquarius, Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces
- **Sextiles** (complementary elements, 60°): Good chemistry — Fire+Air, Earth+Water
- **Squares** (same modality, 90°): Growth through tension

## Natal Chart (requires birth time + location)

**12 Houses**: 1-Self, 2-Values, 3-Communication, 4-Home, 5-Creativity, 6-Health, 7-Partnerships, 8-Transformation, 9-Philosophy, 10-Career, 11-Community, 12-Unconscious

**Key Aspects**: Conjunction (0°, 8° orb), Opposition (180°, 8°), Trine (120°, 6°), Square (90°, 6°), Sextile (60°, 4°)
